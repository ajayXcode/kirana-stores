HOW UWDAARBUDDY WORKS

Three simple steps to digitize your grocery shop's credit management

🎤 Speak or Type Entry

Tap the mic and say "Mohan ne 500 ka udhaar liya" in normal Hindi or English. Or just type it. The app understands both.

📊 Immutable Khata Book

Every entry is saved with full audit trail. Corrections create new rows — nothing is ever deleted. Balances calculated automatically.

📞 Smart Recovery Calls

Send polite automated calls to customers who owe money. They press 1/2 to promise payment, 3 for dispute, 0 to opt-out — all consent-gated and safe.
